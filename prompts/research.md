Research via the researcher subagent: $@

1. Frame the question: scope (venues, years, depth) and what the digest is
   for — a lecture, the learner's reading list, your own decision.
2. Check .pi/todos for earlier research notes (tag research); cite them in
   the task so the subagent extends them instead of repeating the search.
3. Call the subagent tool, single mode, agent `researcher`. Task text: the
   question, the constraints, the purpose, and citations of prior digests.
4. Present the answer and synthesis; record the full digest to the todo
   ledger (tag research) so lectures and later searches cite it by id.
